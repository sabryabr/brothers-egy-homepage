Our story

What began as one car on the Red Sea coast grew — slowly, carefully — into a trusted name across Egypt.

Brothers EGY has stayed a family business on purpose. We answer our own phones, we deliver our own cars, and we remember the people we drive. That is why travellers come back, and why they send their friends.

Today you will find us wherever you need to be — on the Red Sea, along the Mediterranean, in the heart of the capital, and in the quiet streets of Rosetta.
